# v0.4.10 functional logic audit

**Integrated source** means a user action or live callback reaches the listed
logic and its output changes capture, persistence, or reconstruction behavior.
**Unverified** means the behavior still requires a connected physical phone.

| Activity                               | Status                     | Executable call path                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
|----------------------------------------|----------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Landscape application                  | Integrated source          | Manifest locks `MainActivity` to landscape; sensor and preview coordinates are remapped for display rotation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Runtime camera qualification           | Integrated source          | `MainActivity -> CameraQualifier.qualify -> CaptureProfile`; manual sensor, focus lock, a supported `SurfaceTexture` preview size, efficient YUV, maximum exposed Bayer RAW, realtime sensor timestamps, intrinsics, controllable OIS/hot-pixel mode, and uncorrected RAW lens shading are mandatory. Preview and YUV sizes are selected independently from their advertised outputs. Manual WB affects processed live streams and is capability-reported, but never determines RAW qualification. Default and maximum-resolution stream maps are evaluated separately.                                                                                |
| Live setting recommendation            | Integrated source          | Auto AE/AWB/AF results -> exposure/ISO/aperture compensation -> up to three lens-settled RAW-only focus probes -> registered stepped-gray edge-spread selection -> `onSettingsSuggested` accept/override dialog. An override repeats RAW focus validation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Session-wide depth-of-field selection  | Integrated source          | `SessionFocusSelector` brackets the metered focus with at most three supported diopter values. `SteppedGrayDepthOfFieldEvaluator` detects the existing equal gray-step ring boundaries without hard-coded radii, rejects boundary sets whose robust local contrasts do not agree with that equal-increment pattern, measures registered 10–90% edge widths around their visible circumference, associates every sample with camera-space depth from exact RAW `R,t`, and selects the candidate with the smallest 90th-percentile width. At least two boundaries, 20 samples, a 20 mm sampled depth span, and 0.45 equal-step consistency are required. |
| Manual radiometric/focus lock          | Integrated source          | `lockManualSettings -> applyManualControls`; ISO, exposure, the RAW-validated focus, aperture when available, OIS, RAW hot-pixel mode, and black-level behavior are locked once for both camera/view sets. WB/edge/noise/tone controls apply only to processed YUV preview. A focus without stepped-gray calibration cannot be locked.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Independent live/still resolution      | Integrated source          | `createSession` configures a direct `PRIVATE` display surface and roughly 1280×960 YUV guidance stream while every empty-mat or artifact still pauses both live targets and issues a RAW-only request at the largest exposed RAW_SENSOR size. Every validation frame is derived from its exact RAW buffer.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| RAW preservation and TIFF development  | Integrated source          | `RawFrameProcessor` first copies the RAW plane verbatim, then streams an uncompressed 16-bit linear RGB TIFF row-by-row using recorded black/white levels and deterministic bilinear demosaicing. No second full-resolution RGB frame is retained in RAM.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Effective pipeline audit               | Integrated source          | Each RAW `TotalCaptureResult` is checked against locked sensor values and applicable RAW-affecting controls. Session-to-session ISO, exposure, frame duration, focus, and aperture changes reject the frame. Processed-stream controls are excluded from RAW requests rather than represented as RAW processing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Live viewfinder                        | Integrated source          | Camera2 writes continuously to a `TextureView` surface independently of OpenCV. A transparent reticle overlays that surface. YUV analysis is capped at 10 Hz and cannot stall display delivery.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Live-frame memory bound                | Integrated source          | `FramePlanes.copyOf` packs only the visible Y/U/V samples and discards row/pixel padding. `grayMat` writes the already-packed luminance plane directly; the removed YUV-to-BGR display conversion no longer creates repeated full-frame Java and native display buffers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Saddle/subpixel target localization    | Integrated source          | `MatPoseProcessor.detectTargets -> ellipseCandidates` bounds proposal search to a 1280 px working edge, fits projectively foreshortened target contours, normalizes samples in ellipse coordinates, retains Hough circles only as a frontal fallback, then applies full-resolution `cornerSubPix` only when its complete search window is in bounds. Invalid proposals return no observation; the temporary point matrix is released even when OpenCV rejects refinement.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Coded A–H identity                     | Integrated source          | each circular ROI is sampled by quadrant away from its borders/cross -> frame color planes to RGB -> adaptive black/white plus explicit yellow/cyan symbols -> one shared quarter-turn hypothesis for the complete printed layout -> target scale/ring grouping -> geometric A–H candidates -> PnP selection. A target's quadrants are never averaged into one color, and each target is resolved from its full printed code, scale, ring, and layout configuration.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| RAW analysis color                     | Integrated source          | The bounded exact-RAW analysis copy applies gains derived from `SENSOR_NEUTRAL_COLOR_POINT` plus an sRGB display transfer for stable yellow/cyan/black/white decoding. The gains are recorded and are not applied to the verbatim RAW master or linear TIFF.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Metric six-DoF pose                    | Integrated source          | planar IPPE hypotheses -> LM PnP refinement -> metric camera center and `R,t`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Prior-frame tracking                   | Integrated source          | LK optical flow propagates fiducials; failed/aged tracks return to full detection.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Pose smoothing                         | Integrated source          | `PoseKalmanSmoother` produces the live guidance copy only; the unsmoothed still-frame PnP measurement is preserved for reconstruction.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Empty-mat negative reference           | Integrated source          | calibration button -> eight sequential RAW-only exposures -> bounded RAW-derived registration frames -> canonical template persisted on-device. YUV cannot enter the template.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Negative-space segmentation            | Integrated source          | radiometric template fit/subtraction -> connected artifact component -> image/canonical masks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Load reduction from negative space     | Integrated source          | intermittent live `IncrementalGeometryEngine.integrate` supports guidance only. Reconstruction uses the mat's known 150 × 150 mm footprint and a conservative 0–150 mm height, so processed YUV state never bounds model geometry. RAW-derived masks constrain stereo/fusion.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Selectable motion model                | Integrated source          | mode spinner persists `CAMERA_ORBIT` or `TURNTABLE`; live instructions change while PnP measures either camera motion or rigid mat/artifact rotation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Persistent user-relative guidance zero | Integrated source          | setup button freezes phone-attitude and scene-pose guidance baselines plus the guidance artifact origin; all remain fixed until activity teardown.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Guidance/processing separation         | Integrated source          | `PhoneSensorTracker.userRelative` and filtered PnP feed only `GuidedCaptureCoordinator`; reconstruction reads exact still-frame `K,R,t`, never a baseline-adjusted or filtered pose.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Two-pass coverage                      | Integrated source          | `OrbitController` runs upper 18 × 20° from 0°, then lower 18 × 20° from 10°; total 36 and combined 10° rotational positions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Expected next frame                    | Integrated source          | target band + current working distance + hull envelope -> `NextViewPredictor`; projected fiducials are compared with live observations.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Entire mat in every frame              | Integrated source          | `MatVisibilityEvaluator` projects 72 points on the known 75 mm perimeter and requires a 12 px minimum frame margin. Printed numbers are not read.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Automatic quality gate                 | Integrated source          | guidance position/tilt/roll, PnP RMSE, expected-view RMSE, overlap, full-mat margin, artifact Laplacian sharpness, live stepped-gray focus score, radiometry, artifact confidence, and four-frame stability -> `captureStill`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Exact RAW depth-of-field audit         | Integrated source          | Each accepted RAW still produces a bounded linear green-channel focus image directly from black-subtracted/white-normalized Bayer values, without neutral gains, gamma, tone mapping, sharpening, or denoising. Visible gray-step edges outside the artifact mask must preserve the equal-step contrast pattern and score at least 0.80 against the locked session calibration; the analysis and score are written to metadata and required by the durable manifest commit.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| View-dependent artifact silhouette     | Integrated source          | negative-space masks change normally across views; the former fixed-footprint-IoU rejection path has been removed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Exact still-frame validation           | Integrated source          | still capture pauses repeating preview and issues only a RAW target. A bounded validation frame generated from that exact RAW exposure independently supplies unsmoothed pose, containment, mask, sharpness, radiometry, and clipping checks in every pixel mode.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Synchronized exposure record           | Integrated source          | one sensor timestamp keys the RAW master, RAW-derived TIFF, exact pose/mask, and quaternion SLERP between bracketing rotation-vector samples -> RAW/TIFF/mask/JSON + atomic session manifest. No nearest-preview pose fallback remains.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Automatic project autosave             | Integrated source          | `CaptureSessionStore` atomically updates schema-8 `session.json` after camera/focus lock, RAW empty-mat calibration, guidance zero, every idempotent frame commit, pass transitions, capture completion, and reconstruction start/pause/failure/completion. A frame enters the project only after timestamp-consistent RAW, TIFF, mask, metadata, exact pose, passing stepped-gray focus evidence, `cameraIndex`, and schedule fields are durable.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Project reopening                      | Integrated source          | startup offers the newest compatible saved project; schema 6 is migrated from `roundIndex` to `cameraIndex`, while older projects remain on disk but are skipped because they lack mandatory RAW-authoritative capture geometry. The app restores its mat template, mode, capture count, exact next schedule target, reconstruction eligibility, and completed PLY. Continuing capture requires a fresh user-relative sensor zero.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Reconstruction recovery                | Integrated source          | schema-3 reconstruction checkpoints preserve refined camera poses, sparse voxel state, and every successful dense pair. Project autosave records the visible stage and pause/failure reason; pressing reconstruct resumes completed dense work.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Measured geometry in reconstruction    | Integrated source          | `CaptureSessionStore.frames` loads exact still-frame intrinsics/extrinsics. Scheduled target index/azimuth are loaded separately for match planning; they never replace measured `K[R                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |t]`, and the guidance baseline remains excluded. |
| Schedule-driven candidate graph        | Integrated source          | `ScheduleMatchPlanner` replays capture order from the fixed 0°/20° upper and 10°/20° lower grids. Each lower view gets all four ±10°/±30° accumulated upper candidates; no reactive nearest-measured-azimuth selection or tie-break remains.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Photogrammetric camera identity        | Integrated source          | `cameraIndex = 0` groups the upper view set and `cameraIndex = 1` groups the lower view set throughout persistence, match planning, sparse refinement, and dense pairing. Upper/lower rounds remain guidance state rather than reconstruction identity.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Accumulated sparse feature matching    | Integrated source          | foreground-masked SIFT is cached once per frame -> mutual L2 0.72 ratio -> fundamental-matrix RANSAC -> disjoint-set merge into multi-image tracks. The required bridge is six successful cross-camera edges and 20 accepted cross-camera tracks, not one partner for every image.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Full-set post-pass                     | Integrated source          | after both cameras exist, untried schedule-supported edges out to 70° are attempted under a 36-comparison budget, including one wider cross-camera candidate per 60° sector; the complete graph is then rebuilt into tracks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Metric multi-view DLT                  | Integrated source          | every accepted track uses all of its observations with measured `K[R                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |t]` matrices -> homogeneous SVD triangulation -> cheirality/reprojection filters. |
| Global pose/point refinement           | Integrated source, limited | `GlobalTrackRefiner` alternates multi-view track DLT with per-camera OpenCV LM updates using all tracks visible in that camera. Pose changes are bounded to exact mat-PnP priors and checkpointed. This is global data sharing, but not one joint Ceres normal-equation solve.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Dense depth and fusion                 | Integrated source          | schedule-adjacent views with globally refined/checkpointed poses -> calibrated rectification -> hull-bounded SGBM -> mask/AABB/Z filters -> metric voxel fusion.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Resource constraints                   | Integrated source, limited | `AndroidResourceProbe -> ResourceGovernor` gates every capture, sparse/refinement entry, every dense pair, and export; dense work honors throttled resolution and checkpoints. Sparse work is bounded but does not yet consume every throttle budget or re-probe inside the stage.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Metric RGB PLY                         | Integrated source          | fused points already in the mat's millimeter frame -> RGB/confidence binary PLY -> Android `FileProvider` share.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Umeyama similarity registration        | Intentionally absent       | Mat PnP already fixes scale, axes, and plane datum in millimeters for every frame. Re-solving a free 7-DoF similarity is not needed for this implementation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
